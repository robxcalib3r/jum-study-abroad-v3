# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: Prospective students from Bangladesh—with particular representation and community roots among indigenous Jumma students from the Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT)—seeking Bachelor’s or Master’s degrees abroad.
- Secondary: Parents and guardians in Bangladesh navigating overseas higher education financing, scholarship feasibility, and visa security.

## Product Purpose
Demystify and streamline the journey to studying abroad. The product provides clear, honest, and accessible educational consulting, replacing confusion and predatory agent fees with transparent timelines, university matching, scholarship support, and reliable visa preparation. Success means students gain admission and visa approval for reputable international universities without falling victim to misleading promises.

## Positioning
Honest, community-rooted educational guidance centered on accessible, English-taught European universities (emphasizing tuition-free or low-tuition public universities in France, Germany, Poland, Hungary, Romania, and the UK) combined with thorough visa interview preparation and scholarship curation.

## Operating Context
- Students and parents researching abroad options primarily on mobile phones and desktop browsers in Bangladesh.
- High-stakes, anxiety-inducing visa interview and documentation processes (embassy interviews, blocked accounts, financial sponsorship papers).
- Information asymmetry and predatory consulting practices prevalent in South Asian study-abroad markets.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-page advisory web portal with responsive navigation: Destinations, Services, About Us, Resources (guides/articles), FAQs, and Consultation Booking.
- Direct consultation booking and inquiry lead capture via email and phone channels.
- Verified destination database with country profiles and admission details.
- Technical constraints: Vite React SPA with client-side routing, static content collections (Decap CMS friendly), deployed to Cloudflare Workers / Pages.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Jum Study Abroad
- Voice: Friendly, transparent, encouraging, grounded, and authoritative without academic pretentiousness.
- Core Values: Integrity, transparency, student-first advocacy, and cultural pride and inclusion for indigenous Jumma communities.
- Established Identity: Professional blue palette (`#1a73e8`), clean typography (Inter / Poppins), authentic student profiles and verifiable outcomes.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real student alumni profiles and testimonials (`src/content/testimonials/`, `src/components/Hero.tsx` candidate records): Dipika Tripura, Binoy Chakma, Shima Marma, Ananta Tripura.
- Documented statistics: 97% success rate, 1,200+ students guided, 15+ countries covered.
- Curated country guides and articles (`src/content/destinations/`, `src/content/articles/`).
- Verified local contact presence: Dhaka, Bangladesh (`admissions@jumstudyabroad.com`).

## Product Principles
1. **Radical Transparency**: Clear timelines, realistic cost estimates, and honest assessment of admissions chances; never make false visa guarantees or hide fees.
2. **Accessible Ambition**: Champion high-quality European universities and scholarship opportunities that make world-class education viable without crippling debt.
3. **Step-by-Step Empowerment**: Break the overwhelming admissions and visa bureaucracy into structured, manageable milestones.
4. **Community Trust & Respect**: Honor student stories and backgrounds authentically, building trust through real peer success.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Designed for universal web accessibility (WCAG AA targets): high-contrast typography, screen-reader friendly semantic structure, legible form inputs, and fast loading over variable bandwidth connections in Bangladesh.
